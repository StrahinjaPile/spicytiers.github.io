Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about SpicyTiers.

What is SpicyPVP? +

SpicyPVP is a Minecraft mod that basically adds ranked and unranked queues and an ELO system that allows you to progress through tiers, similar to competitive games such as Fortnite and CS2.

How do I get the mod? +

You will be able to download the SpicyTiers client from the Client page once it is released.

Can I become a staff member? +

For a limited period of time, you will be able to apply for staff through our Discord server.

Do you have other questions? +

If you have another question, open a ticket on our Discord and our staff will help you.
