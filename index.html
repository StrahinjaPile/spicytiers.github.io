ABOUT SPICYTIERS

Built for competitive Minecraft PvP.

SpicyTiers is a Client that introduces queueing instantly from the home screen. And even better, the tiering system will be completely ELO based for a much better experience.

01

Players are matched with opponents close to their skill level. For example, a player with 1400 ELO could be matched with players around 1350–1450 ELO.

02

20 TPS Servers

We are building the network around stable, competitive servers and working towards extremely low input delay.

03

Anti-Cheat

We are developing our own anti-cheat designed to detect a wide range of cheats and keep competitive matches fair.

04

ELO Based Ranking

Your performance actually matters. Win matches, gain ELO and climb through the SpicyTiers ranking system.
