Rules

Follow these rules to keep SpicyTiers fair and enjoyable for everyone.

01 — Cheating

Any form of cheating, hacked clients, unfair modifications or other software that provides an unfair advantage is prohibited.

02 — Exploiting

Exploiting bugs or unintended mechanics to gain an unfair advantage is not allowed.

03 — Abuse

Do not abuse matchmaking, ELO systems or other SpicyTiers systems.

04 — Sportsmanship

Keep the community respectful. Harassment, threats and targeted abuse are not tolerated.
